Speed Cams Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Speed Cams warns you when you approach a speed camera. It is built to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Location

The app uses your device's location, including in the background if you allow "Always" access, to measure your distance and speed relative to nearby speed cameras. This processing happens entirely on your device. Your location and speed are not sent to us and are not stored anywhere except temporarily in the app's memory and on-device storage.

Camera data downloads

To know where cameras are, the app downloads public camera location files for the area around you (squares roughly 70 miles across). These requests go to:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the camera files, and
  • if those files are unavailable, the public OpenStreetMap Overpass API.

Like any website, these servers can see your IP address and which area's files were requested, and may keep standard server logs under their own privacy policies (GitHub, OpenStreetMap Foundation). We do not receive or store this information.

Maps

The map is provided by Apple Maps on iPhone and Google Maps on Android. When you view the map, the map provider receives the map area being displayed, under Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

Notifications and audio

Camera warnings are generated on your device as local notifications and spoken alerts. No push notification service or server is involved.

Purchases (Speed Cams Pro)

Pro purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. To check whether Pro is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history, a random anonymous identifier created by the app, and basic device information (such as app version, OS version and country/store region). RevenueCat does not receive your location. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Data we collect

We do not collect your location, and we do not sell or share personal information. The only data processed on our behalf is the purchase information described above, used solely to provide Pro features you've paid for.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
